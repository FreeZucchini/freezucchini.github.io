POWER ELECTRONICS

Lipo Charger

A PCB capable of charging 6 small Lipo's simultaneously.

THE PROJECT

What was it?

During our annual pico-satellite competition teams would charge their batteries one at a time. This was inefficient and teams lost precious time to test their payloads before launch. I developed this PCB to charge 6-lipo's at once so all the teams could charge their batteries at once.

TECHNICAL CONCEPTS

What was involved?

  • Power Delivery ICs
  • Circular PCB Design
  • High Power PCB Routing
  • High Power Load Testing
  • Battery Charging ICs

MY CONTRIBUTION

What I did

I came up with the circuit design, researched into Power Delivery ICs and choose the most suitable one. After finalizing on the components I designed the entire PCB, ordered it and assembled it for testing.

ENGINEERING CHALLENGE

A challenge I faced

I was having trouble routing the USB-C connector to the PD IC in Altium. I was struggling to route this part of the board while maintaining the signal integrity of the Channel Configuration (CC) signal. To solve this problem took a bit of trail and error, many hours and a compromise of using 2 vias to route a CC line but the routing was completed well in the end.

TAKEAWAY

What I learned

This project was my first introduction to Power Electronics both in terms of circuit and PCB design. I really enjoyed learning about different USB protocols when looking into the USB C connector, and I also got a chance to broaden my PCB design experience by routing a circular PCB using the Polar Grid in Altium.
