SPACE SYSTEMS

Radiation Detector

A space-rated radiation detection payload developed for the APSS-3 CubeSat.

THE PROJECT

What was it?

I used an SiPM to detect UV light which is emitted by a crystal every time it detects radiation. The SiPM is biased by 29V using a DC-DC boost converter. After the SiPM detects the UV light, this weak signal is first passed through a high pass filter and then amplified. The amplified signal is passed into a peak detector which holds the voltage long enough for the ADC on our MSP430 microcontroller to register that radiation has been detected.

TECHNICAL CONCEPTS

What was involved?

  • Silicon photomultiplier
  • Analogue signal conditioning
  • Peak detection
  • DC-DC boost conversion
  • I²C / SPI
  • Embedded systems

MY CONTRIBUTION

What I did

I worked on the analogue signal-processing circuitry and PCB design. I also assembled and tested the PCB successfully. The PCB was assembled using a hot plate and heat gun and tested using a bench power supply, multimeter and oscilloscope.

ENGINEERING CHALLENGE

A challenge I faced

During testing, I had issues getting the op-amp to output the correct amplified voltage. I went component by component and traced out the issue to a short between two pins of the opamp. Another issue I encountered is the I2C transmission for the temperature sensor timing-out. I fixed this by testing the TI I2C libraries on a test sensor and porting that code to this PCB.

TAKEAWAY

What I learned

This project strengthened my understanding of analogue signal conditioning as the board features an amplifier and peak detector. I also learned how to route a DC-DC boost converter and how to debug communication protocols like I2C and SPI.
