SPACE SYSTEMS

Radiation Detector

A space-rated radiation detection payload developed for the APSS-3 CubeSat.

THE PROJECT

What was it?

The radiation detector was designed to measure radiation events using a silicon photomultiplier and analogue signal-processing circuitry. The payload was developed for use on the APSS-3 CubeSat.

TECHNICAL CONCEPTS

What was involved?

  • Analogue signal processing
  • Silicon photomultipliers
  • Op-amp circuits
  • PCB design
  • Signal conditioning
  • Embedded systems

MY CONTRIBUTION

What I did

I worked on the analogue signal-processing circuitry, component selection and PCB design. I also contributed to hardware testing and debugging to verify that the detector behaved as expected.

ENGINEERING CHALLENGE

A challenge I faced

During testing, the detector output contained unexpected noise. I investigated the signal path and hardware configuration to identify possible sources before modifying and retesting the circuit.

TAKEAWAY

What I learned

This project strengthened my understanding of analogue signal conditioning and low-level measurements. It also taught me to approach hardware debugging systematically using measured behaviour rather than relying solely on simulation.
