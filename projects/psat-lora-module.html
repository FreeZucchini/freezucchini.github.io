WIRELESS COMMUNICATION

PSAT LoRa Module

A custom LoRa radio PCB designed for a pico-satellite.

THE PROJECT

What was it?

APSS annually hosts a competition where we design pico-satellites (PSATs). For my satellite, I designed a small, low-power LoRa PCB that communicated with a ground station, allowing us to track our PSAT during its descent.

The end result was fantastic: we launched our payload on a small rocket and tracked our PSAT as it descended on a parachute from an altitude of 1.5 km. Our LoRa communication system allowed my team to locate our PSAT faster than the other teams.

TECHNICAL CONCEPTS

What was involved?

  • LoRa Wireless Communication
  • RF PCB Design
  • PCB Layout
  • Embedded C
  • RF Optimisation
  • Hardware Testing

MY CONTRIBUTION

What I did

I designed the schematic and PCB layout for the LoRa radio module, keeping in mind the unique physical constraints of our PSAT, which was shaped like a tear drop to reduce aerodynamic drag.

I then assembled and tested the PCB and wrote embedded C to interface with the LoRa radio. After confirming that the PCB worked, my team carried out field testing of the communication system and fine-tuned the communication parameters to optimise its transmission range while maintaining a practical data rate.

ENGINEERING CHALLENGE

A challenge I faced

Designing a PCB for a wireless communication system introduced constraints that I had not encountered before, particularly as a first-year student. I had to consider the layout of the radio circuitry while also fitting the board within the physical constraints of the satellite.

This made me realise that wireless communication hardware involves more than simply designing a circuit that functions electrically. The physical implementation of the PCB can affect the performance of the communication system, particularly when dealing with RF signals. This made careful PCB layout an important part of the overall design.

TAKEAWAY

What I learned

This was one of my first PCB projects and taught me the importance of test points, careful PCB layout and designing around physical constraints.

This project was also where my interest in radio technology began. It made me want to understand wireless communication in greater depth, particularly the relationship between RF circuitry, PCB layout, antennas and real-world communication performance.
